DevOps Support Engineer
EnterpriseSystems Inc. - Remote

About EnterpriseSystems:
We provide enterprise software solutions to Fortune 500 companies. Our DevOps team supports thousands of customer deployments.

The Role:
Join our DevOps support team to help customers with their deployments and technical issues. You'll be the first line of support for our platform.

Day-to-Day:
- Respond to customer tickets in Zendesk queue
- Troubleshoot deployment issues for customers
- Escalate complex issues to engineering
- Update knowledge base articles
- Join customer calls to explain technical details
- Meet SLA targets for ticket response times

Requirements:
- 3+ years in technical support or DevOps
- Experience with ticket queue management
- Customer-facing communication skills
- Basic knowledge of containers and cloud
- Ability to work in shifts (some weekend coverage required)

Metrics:
- Ticket resolution rate
- Customer satisfaction scores
- SLA compliance

This role is focused on supporting customers, not building internal systems. You'll work with existing tools, not design new platforms.

Compensation: $100,000 - $125,000
Full-time, permanent, remote position

Our support team uses Kubernetes and AWS, but you'll be triaging issues, not building infrastructure.
