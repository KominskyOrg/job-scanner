Senior Platform Engineer
TechCorp Inc. - Remote

About the Role:
We're looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to own and evolve our Kubernetes-based platform infrastructure. You'll be responsible for designing and implementing infrastructure patterns that enable our engineering teams to deploy reliably at scale.

Responsibilities:

- Design and maintain Terraform modules for multi-account AWS infrastructure
- Build and improve our observability stack using Datadog (APM, logs, metrics)
- Lead incident response and drive reliability improvements through postmortems
- Define platform standards and best practices for the engineering organization
- Implement GitOps workflows using ArgoCD and Helm

Requirements:
- 5+ years of experience in platform/infrastructure engineering
- Deep expertise with Kubernetes (preferably EKS) in production environments
- Strong Terraform skills with multi-account AWS experience
- Experience with observability platforms (Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana)
- Track record of reducing cloud costs and improving system reliability

Nice to have:
- Experience with service mesh (Istio, Linkerd)
- Go or Python programming experience
- SRE certifications

Work arrangement: Fully remote (US-based)
Compensation: $150,000 - $180,000 base + equity

This is a full-time, permanent position with comprehensive benefits.
