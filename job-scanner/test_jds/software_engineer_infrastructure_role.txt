Software Engineer, Infrastructure
ScaleUp Technologies - Remote

The Role:

What You'll Do:

- Drive incident response and on-call rotations
- Architect solutions for scaling our platform to 10x current load

What We're Looking For:
- 5+ years of experience with production infrastructure
- Expert-level Kubernetes experience (cluster management, networking, security)
- Strong Terraform and AWS skills (EKS, RDS, S3, IAM)
- Experience with GitOps (ArgoCD, Flux) and CI/CD pipelines
- Demonstrated ownership of reliability and cost optimization

Benefits:

- Competitive salary ($160k-$190k) + equity
- Full-time permanent position

We're looking for a Senior IC to drive infrastructure forward, not manage a team.
